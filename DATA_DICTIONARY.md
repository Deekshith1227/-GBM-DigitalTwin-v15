\# Clinical Variables



\## Age



Description:

Age of patient at diagnosis.



Data Type:

Numeric



Unit:

Years



Example:

55



Role:

Clinical Prognostic Variable



References:

Widely used in glioblastoma prognostic models.



\---



\## Grade



Description:

WHO Glioma Grade.



Data Type:

Categorical



Allowed Values:

2

3

4



Role:

Tumor Severity Indicator



References:

WHO CNS Tumor Classification.



\---



\## IDH



Description:

IDH mutation status.



Data Type:

Binary



Allowed Values:

0 = Wild Type

1 = Mutant



Role:

Molecular Prognostic Biomarker



References:

WHO CNS Tumor Classification.



\---



\## MGMT



Description:

MGMT promoter methylation status.



Data Type:

Binary



Allowed Values:

0 = Unmethylated

1 = Methylated



Role:

Predictive and Prognostic Biomarker



References:

Temozolomide response studies.

\# Model Output Variables



\## Risk Score



Description:

Continuous risk score generated by the Random Survival Forest model.



Data Type:

Numeric



Output:

Higher values indicate increased mortality risk.



\---



\## Risk Group



Description:

Patient stratification based on median risk threshold.



Allowed Values:

HIGH RISK

LOW RISK



Threshold:

2.722561579039131



\---



\## Survival Probability



Description:

Predicted probability of survival at specific time points.



Outputs:

1-Year Survival

3-Year Survival

5-Year Survival



Unit:

Percentage
